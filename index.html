About Beljo Corporation

Beljo Corporation is a premier Liberian integrated services firm specializing in Civil Engineering, Vertical Construction, and Strategic Procurement.

  • Large-scale infrastructure and structural development projects.
  • Government-compliant procurement and sourcing solutions.
  • Reliable delivery for Government Institutions, Commercial Developers, and NGOs across Liberia.

Our Mission

To deliver technically precise, cost-efficient, and compliant engineering, construction, and procurement solutions that support Liberia’s development.

  • Maintain the highest standards of quality and safety.
  • Ensure transparency and regulatory compliance.
  • Build lasting partnerships through integrity and professionalism.
  • Promote sustainable and responsible project execution.

Our Core Values

  • Integrity – We operate with transparency and accountability.
  • Excellence – We deliver high-quality results with technical precision.
  • Compliance – We strictly adhere to regulatory standards.

Our values guide every project we undertake and every partnership we build.

Why Choose Beljo Corporation

We combine engineering expertise, procurement compliance, and disciplined project execution to deliver dependable results.

  • Experienced technical and management team.
  • Strong understanding of public and private sector requirements.
  • Commitment to quality, safety, and timely completion.
